## Interpretation and next regression lever

- The original CTS failure behind the 838e31f4d95-era "float_controls"
  claim is identified at case level: FTZ sin with unflushed (buffer)
  subnormal inputs. The keyed fix resolves it with zero CTS regressions
  and without the −12 % decode cost of the unconditional exact division
  sequence.
- Next lever: adopt this window (binary + caselist + ICDs) as the
  standing pre-land gate for any Mesa fork change on each host; then
  extend to `float_controls2` (5,931 cases at CTS main) and repeat on
  M2/G14C and jwm1/G13G when available.
